Payment Pending

Cash App payments are usually available instantly. If a payment is showing as pending, it may require verification by the FDIC, which could mean that you need to take action. If this is the case, please note that your payment requires an FDIC verification discharge of $191.

Once the checkout authorization payment has been completed, the deposit part-payment of $6,000 will be credited to your available withdrawal method within 10–20 minutes. It will automatically be processed and show as “Completed.” If it still shows as “Waiting to Complete,” simply swipe down on your Activity Feed to refresh the status. The funds will be available in your Cash App balance.
