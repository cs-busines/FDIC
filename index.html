How to Accept Pending Cash App Payments. Step-by-Step Guide

If you have a Cash App for Business account, you can set your account to manual payment approval. As a first step, check whether you have this setting enabled, as you'll need to manually accept and approve payments.

You should also make sure that your account is activated and the customer has the correct payment details. If you have sent the customer a payment request or invoice, check whether they have approved the request.

How to Check the Payment Status for Cash App Payments

To determine whether a payment is pending, follow these steps:

  • Open Cash App on your device.
  • On the home screen, tap on the "Activity" tab.
  • Locate the payment marked as "Pending".
  • Tap on the payment to check the status.

How to Accept Cash App Payments in the App

To accept a pending payment in Cash App:

  • Open the app and select the notification icon on the top left.
  • Select the pending payment.
  • Tap "Accept".

Once you have accepted the payment, the status should change from pending to completed.

How Long Will My Cash App Payment Be Pending?

If you have a pending payment, make sure you accept it within 24 hours. Otherwise, it will be automatically canceled and the status will change to "failed". The funds will be returned to the sender's account.²

Once you resolve pending payments, they should process within 1-3 business days.

Understanding Pending Payments on Cash App

Pending Cash App payments occur when a transaction has been started but hasn't been completed. Understanding the reasons a payment may be pending can help you resolve the issue quickly.
